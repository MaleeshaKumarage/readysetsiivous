# ReadySetSiivous — brand foundations

Every token below was sampled directly from `public/logo.webp`: **deep navy ink** and **warm gold**. They are codified in `tailwind.config.js` as the `accent` (navy) and `brand` (gold) scales, and mirrored in `tokens.ts` as plain values.

## Color — navy (the logo’s ink)

<SwatchRow>
  {Object.entries(palette.navy).map(([name, hex]) => (
    <Swatch key={name} name={name} hex={hex} />
  ))}
</SwatchRow>

The **logo navy `#0a1741`** is the brand’s dark base — use it for primary buttons, ink cards, and dark surfaces.

## Color — gold (the logo’s accent)

<SwatchRow>
  {Object.entries(palette.gold).map(([name, hex]) => (
    <Swatch key={name} name={name} hex={hex} />
  ))}
</SwatchRow>

Gold is an *accent*, never a text color on white: it pairs with navy text (`text-accent-950` on `bg-brand-500`), exactly as the logo pairs the two.

## Color — WhatsApp green (CTA)

## Typography

Inter, extrabold for headings with tight tracking:

| Token | Class | Size | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| xl | `.heading-xl` | 3.75rem | 800 |
| lg | `.heading-lg` | 2.25rem | 700 |
| md | `.heading-md` | 1.875rem | 700 |
| sm | `text-xl font-bold` | 1.25rem | 700 |

## Shape & elevation

| Radius | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sm` | 0.75rem | inputs, small cards |
| `md` | 1rem | cards |
| `lg` | 1.5rem | hero imagery |
| `full` | 9999px | buttons, badges |

Elevation is brand-tinted: `navy-glow` (`rgb(13 20 40 / 0.25)`) behind primary buttons, `gold-glow` (`rgb(201 163 64 / 0.35)`) behind gold buttons.
