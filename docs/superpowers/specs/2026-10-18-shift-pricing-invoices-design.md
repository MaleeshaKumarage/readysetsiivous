# Shift pricing and invoices

User authorized implementing the missing invoice feature. Pricing is admin-only and separate from quality reports. Support effective-dated net EUR prices: fixed monthly, scheduled-hourly, and per completed occurrence for on-call shifts. Per-visit overrides require a reason. Methehe biweekly receives EUR150/month before VAT. Prorate partial months by active calendar days; admins may override a shift/month net subtotal with a required reason. Price changes never rewrite issued invoice snapshots.

Extend the existing invoice model, numbering and paid/void lifecycle. Add admin invoice UI, issuer settings, shift pricing dialog and on-call visit price editor. One invoice combines selected shifts from one company over an inclusive tenant-local date range, at most 366 days. Show company/customer billing address, shift name, branch, scheduled hours, occurrence count, billing period, pricing basis, net, VAT breakdown and gross. Monthly rows split by calendar month and effective rate segments; an override is permitted only when that month's rows share one VAT rate. Hourly totals use scheduled visit duration once per visit, never staff count or actual recorded duration. Cancelled visits are excluded; moved/extra visits follow resolved schedule. On-call charges require completed visit evidence, counted once even with several cleaners; missing completion means no charge. Missing pricing is a clear preview error, not free work.

New separate tenant billing documents hold price history and occurrence overrides, avoiding employee response or QC report changes. Use the shared schedule resolver and tenant-local date boundaries, preserving historical branch/active periods. Monthly fee does not depend on completed visit count. Partial active periods exclude inactive days and honor validity dates. Price changes split the month by calendar days. Explicit monthly overrides replace a month subtotal and retain the calculated basis and reason in the snapshot. Existing valid issued/paid invoices block overlapping periods for any selected shift; voiding permits corrected reissue.

Preview is read-only and returns calculated invoice plus deterministic preview token. Issuing recalculates under the existing cross-replica write reservation, checks the token and duplicate periods, then allocates the invoice number and persists a complete snapshot. User changes to selection, address, language or overrides clear preview. Issuer/pricing/schedule changes require preview again. Existing booking invoice routes remain compatible. PDF language en/fi/sv translates system labels only, preserves entered names/reasons and supports long content. No customer email is sent automatically.

Issuer settings update only invoice fields, not tenant identity or timezone. Extract only user-authorized issuer fields from the active agreement; missing address/IBAN/BIC use user-authorized, clearly labelled sample values stored in the database until replaced. Issuing requires configured issuer identity/address/bank details and customer billing address; preview can show incomplete setup. Preserve all unrelated production data during narrowly scoped Methehe price/issuer setup, with private backup and verification. Do not issue a real invoice during tests.

Validation: tenant isolation, role denial, exact money rounding, monthly full/partial/leap days, price effective changes, override reasons, moved/cancelled/extra/overnight visits, DST boundaries, completed on-call visits/multi-cleaner deduplication, missing prices, mixed VAT, overlapping duplicate/concurrent issue, stale preview, saved immutable snapshots, legacy booking PDFs, multilingual/multipage PDF and desktop/mobile preview/issue/error flows.

Calculation policy: .NET decimal throughout, EUR amounts rounded to two decimals with MidpointRounding.AwayFromZero per invoice line. VAT is rounded from each line's final net subtotal using its effective price plan VAT rate; invoice net/VAT/gross sum these rounded amounts. A monthly segment is rate × inclusive active days intersecting selection, rate validity and calendar month ÷ calendar days in that month. EffectiveFrom dates are unique and each plan ends immediately before the next. Group by month/rate/branch. A monthly override collapses same-VAT month segments to one line, preserving summed hours/count and original calendar basis in its description; mixed VAT overrides are rejected.

Duplicate policy: only Issued/Paid invoices containing matching Lines.ShiftId and a non-null invoice PeriodFrom/PeriodTo participate. Existing booking invoices have no shift IDs/period and never block shift invoices. Any overlapping selected period for an already billed shift is rejected; correct by voiding and reissuing. Pricing edits/visit overrides that touch an issued period are rejected. Completion source for on-call is submitted QualityCycleForm records grouped by resolved scheduled StartUtc, all expected roster employee tasks submitted (or any submitted task when no expected roster remains). Count a visit once, irrespective of cleaner count; moved visits use resolved target StartUtc. Preview token hashes normalized invoice inputs/result plus price/schedule/roster/completion source fingerprints; changes between preview and issue require another preview. No form IDs, employee IDs or evidence content are exposed on invoice DTOs.

## Report email and banking barcode follow-up

Report delivery uses a branded navy/gold HTML email from the immutable shared report snapshot. Subject/body identify full reporting months or explicit inclusive ranges (nonconsecutive months stay separate). Included company/branch/shift names, report contents, photo appendix availability, review/download/receipt steps, and the original share expiry in tenant time are shown. Acknowledgment is receipt only. System text supports English, Finnish and Swedish; entered content is preserved and HTML encoded. Retrying delivery keeps the same share and expiry. No invoice email is sent automatically.

Eligible issued EUR invoices with a real Finnish IBAN, national payment reference and supported positive amount/date get a Finance Finland version-4 Code128 Set-C banking barcode and a separate credit-transfer page. Sample issuer details never generate a payable barcode. Draft, paid, voided and unsupported invoices omit it. Long form content stays with the barcode. No external barcode service is used.

Verification: 27 barcode cases passed in an isolated local .NET9 host using production renderer/helper sources; official vectors, eligibility, localization, dimensions and long fields are covered. The rendered barcode was independently decoded from PDF pixels. Five email cases passed using the actual helper/translations/report record declarations and a local timezone shim. Mobile (390px) and desktop (900px) HTML previews have no horizontal overflow; mobile visually inspected. Full application .NET10 CI remains required before merge. User authorized clearly marked sample address/bank settings stored in the database until real details are entered; production seed/deployment are still pending.
